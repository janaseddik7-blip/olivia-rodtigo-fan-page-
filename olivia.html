About olivia

Olivia Rodrigo is a 23-year-old American pop star and actress. She started on Disney shows like Bizaardvark and High School Musical: The Musical: The Series. She became a global music superstar when her debut single "Drivers License" broke streaming records in 2021. She has won Grammy Awards and released three hit albums, including her latest, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love.
